# VOA content policy & safe reuse (review before production)

Official content reuse policy: https://learningenglish.voanews.com/p/6861.html
Official terms: https://learningenglish.voanews.com/p/6021.html
RSS catalog: https://learningenglish.voanews.com/rssfeeds
Level 1 directory (52 lessons): https://learningenglish.voanews.com/p/5644.html
Level 2 directory (30 lessons): https://learningenglish.voanews.com/p/6765.html
Ongoing professionally provided feed: https://www.usagm.gov/work-with-us/content-requests/voa/

The publisher states that content it produces exclusively, including texts and MP3, is public domain, with credit to VOA Learning English; third-party AP/Reuters/AFP or stock media must NOT be treated as public-domain.

Mandatory workflow:
1. Confirm audio + transcript are produced exclusively by VOA, not merely distributed by them.
2. Do not copy images, music, syndicated excerpts, non-VOA footage unless individually licensed.
3. Keep source article URL and date, licensing notes, reviewer identity and attribution.
4. Consider jurisdiction-specific rights and possible logos/trademarks; attribution in text, no VOA branding implying endorsement.
5. If unclear, ask VOA at learningenglish@voanews.com or USAGM; do not automatically publish.
6. Do not bypass a 403/429, robots rules, paywalls, access limits or anti-bot protections.
7. User asked FULL coverage: do not claim the entire historical archive is gathered solely from latest RSS. For professional archive access apply for USAGM Direct; access may require registration and may involve fees.

Attribution example:
'Audio and transcript: VOA Learning English (source article link). English Skill Lab reorganized this material into Dictation exercises. No endorsement implied.'

This package does NOT provide legal advice and does NOT certify every VOA article as freely distributable. It intentionally requires per-item manual verification.
